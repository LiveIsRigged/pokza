## 1. Freemium Model (28% of revenue, $672K year 3)

### Free Tier (Forever Free)

**Access:**
- Create & publish hands (unlimited)
- View all public hands
- Basic replayer
- Follow players
- Like & comment on hands
- Basic search
- Join communities

**Monetization strategy:** Get users hooked, convert to Premium

**Unit economics:**
- CAC: $0 (organic + network effects)
- LTV: $0 (free users subsidize with data + network effects)
- Churn: 20%/month (free users flakey)
- Viral coefficient: 1.2 (each user invites ~1.2 friends)

---

### Premium Tier ($9/month or $99/year)

**Access:**
- Everything in Free +
- Advanced analytics (win rate by position, opponent database)
- Ad-free experience
- Hand tagging & custom labels
- Coaching directory (verified coaches)
- Private group creation (up to 5 groups)
- CSV export (hands data)
- Early access to new features
- 1 free coaching session/month (credits system)

**Target:** Serious players + coaches

**Pricing rationale:**
- $9/month = ~$100/year (impulse purchase)
- Cheaper than PT4 ($60 one-time)
- Cheaper than GTO Wizard ($25/month)
- Lower than Binked premium ($5-10/month, but with less value)

**Conversion metrics:**
- Free → Premium conversion: 5% (industry standard for freemium apps)
- MAU → Conversion: 3-5% depending on retention cohort
- Highest conversion: Players 30+ days old (more invested)

**Retention:**
- Monthly churn: 3% (sticky product, users pay because they use it)
- Annual retention: 70% (re-engagement campaigns)

**Projected users by year:**

| Year | MAU (Free) | Free → Premium (5%) | Premium Users | Premium Subscribers | Premium Revenue |
|------|-----------|-------------------|---------------|--------------------|-----------------|
| 1 | 10,000 | 500 | 300 | $27K | |
| 2 | 100,000 | 5,000 | 3,000 | $27K + $36K | $324K |
| 3 | 400,000 | 20,000 | 12,000 | $108K | $672K |
| 4 | 1,000,000 | 50,000 | 30,000 | $270K | $1,620K |
| 5 | 2,000,000 | 100,000 | 60,000 | $540K | $3,240K |

**Premium CAC:** ~$50 (targeted ads to engaged free users)
**Premium LTV:** $120 (12 months × $9 + coaching credits = higher)
**LTV:CAC ratio:** 2.4x (healthy for SaaS)

## 2. Coaching Marketplace (35% of revenue, $840K year 3)

**The opportunity:** Coaches are high-margin, defensible revenue.

### How it works

**Coaches list themselves:**
- Profile: Rates, specialization, availability
- Verification badge (manual review by Pokza)
- Calendar integration (Calendly, Acuity)
- Session booking (in-app or redirect)
- Reviews from students

**Pricing:**
- Session prices: $30-300/hour (range: beginner to pro coaches)
- Pokza takes: 15-20% commission (industry standard: 15-30%)
- Coach gets: 80-85% of session fee

**Example:**
- Coach charges $100/hour
- Pokza takes $15 (15%)
- Coach keeps $85

**Revenue model:**
- Per-session commission (only pay when session booked)
- No upfront cost to coach (no listing fee)

### Market sizing

**Coaches on platform (year 3):** 500 coaches
- 50% are part-time (work evenings, weekends)
- 50% are full-time

**Sessions per coach per month:**
- Part-time coach: 8 sessions/month (1/week)
- Full-time coach: 40 sessions/month (2/day)

**Average session price:** $80/hour

**Math:**
- 250 part-time × 8 sessions × $80 = $160K/month → Pokza 15% = $24K/month
- 250 full-time × 40 sessions × $80 = $800K/month → Pokza 15% = $120K/month
- Total Pokza coaching revenue: $144K/month = $1.73M/year

**Year 3 projection (more conservative):**
- 200 coaches on platform
- Avg 15 sessions/month per coach (mix of part/full-time)
- Avg $80 per session
- Revenue: 200 × 15 × $80 × 12 × 15% = $432K/year from coaching

**But we also sell:**

### Coaching Bundles (Prepaid Sessions)

Students buy 5-session packs at 10% discount:
- 5 sessions × $100 = $500 (vs. $550 if booked individually)
- Pokza gets 15% = $75/pack
- Higher conversion (commitment increases completion rate)

**Projections:**
- 30% of sessions booked via bundles
- Bundle revenue adds 15% uplift

**Adjusted year 3:** $432K + $65K (bundles) = $497K

**But we add more:** Specialized services

### Premium Coaching Services (Pokza-employed Coaches)

Pokza hires 10 coaches directly (year 3):
- Salary + benefits: $50K/coach/year = $500K cost
- They work 30 hours/week on content + 10 hours/week on private coaching
- Private coaching: 10 coaches × 40 sessions/month × $120 = $480K/month
- Revenue: $480K × 12 × 15% (Pokza's margin) = $864K/year
- But cost is $500K, so net = $364K profit

**This is expensive, but builds moat (exclusive content).**

### Total Coaching Revenue (Year 3)

| Source | Revenue |
|--------|---------|
| Marketplace commission (user coaches) | $497K |
| Premium coaching (Pokza coaches) | $364K (net) |
| **Total** | **$861K** |

**But spreadsheet says $840K in total revenue breakdown, so this is close. Let's use actual calculated $861K.**

## 3. Sponsored Content (15% of revenue, $360K year 3)

**Sponsors:** Poker training companies, affiliate poker sites, poker tools

### Sponsored Hand Posts

**Format:** A "hand" published by sponsor (clearly marked "Sponsored")
- Featured in feed (appears 2-3x per day)
- Replayer + callout: "Learn this spot from Pro Player + [Sponsor]"
- Conversion: Click → Sponsor website (affiliate link)

**Pricing:** $500-2,000 per sponsored hand (1-2 week duration)

**Sponsors (year 3):**
- 3-5 poker training companies
- 3-5 affiliate poker sites
- 2-3 poker tools (solvers, trackers)

**Math:**
- 50 sponsored hands/month (2-3 per day)
- Avg price: $1,000/hand
- Revenue: 50 × $1,000 × 12 = $600K/year

**But we discount aggressively for annual deals:**
- Annual sponsorship: 500 hands/year = $8,000 (vs. $12K if monthly)
- Pokza signs 3 annual sponsors
- Revenue: 3 × $8,000 = $24K (from annual deals, lower rev)
- Plus 30 monthly sponsors × $1,000 × 12 = $360K
- Total: $384K

**Conservative estimate (year 3):** $360K (assumes slower sponsor adoption)

### Sponsored Coaching

Coaches can pay for "featured" status:
- Premium placement in coach directory
- Featured in email newsletter
- 50+ applicants/month benefit

**Pricing:** $200/month per coach

**Adoption:** 50 coaches × $200 × 12 = $120K/year

**But this is small compared to hand sponsorships.**

### Total Sponsored Revenue (Year 3)

- Sponsored hands: $360K
- Sponsored coaching: $120K (optional, small)
- **Total: $360K-480K** (use $360K conservative)

## 5. Creator Revenue Share (10% of revenue, $240K year 3)

**Pokza pays creators for top content** (similar to YouTube).

### How it works

- Creators earn per view: $0.01-0.05 per view (depending on region)
- Threshold: 10,000 views/month to be eligible
- Minimum: $50/month payout

**Why do this?**
- Incentivize best creators to stay
- Competitive advantage vs. Binked (which has no revenue share)
- Creates moat (creators depend on Pokza for income)

### Creator Economics

**Tiers:**

| Views/Month | CPM ($/1000 views) | Monthly Earnings |
|------------|-------------------|------------------|
| 10K | $2 | $20 |
| 50K | $3 | $150 |
| 100K | $4 | $400 |
| 500K+ | $5 | $2,500+ |

**Pokza's cost:** CPM × views / 1000

**Year 3 Creators:**
- 100 eligible creators (>10K views/month)
- Avg views: 50K/month per creator
- Avg payout: $150-200/creator
- Total: 100 × $175 = $17.5K/month = $210K/year

**Scaling (year 4):**
- 300 eligible creators
- Avg 80K views
- Avg payout: $300
- Total: 300 × $300 = $90K/month = $1.08M/year (high cost)

**Year 3 conservative:** $240K (assumes 120 creators avg $200/month)

**Note:** This is Pokza's cost. Not revenue, but expense. In the breakdown, we include it as "revenue to creators" which is part of the business model philosophy. For net revenue, subtract from margin.

## 6. Partnerships & Data Licensing (4% of revenue, $96K year 3)

**Pokza licenses anonymized hand data to partners.**

### Partnership Types

### A. Poker Site Partnerships (PokerStars, GGPoker, 888)

**Model:** Revenue share on referred players
- Player imports hands from PokerStars to Pokza
- Pokza shares referral credit (if player plays more)
- Pokza gets 2-5% rake share from referred players

**Scale (year 3):**
- 50,000 hands imported from PokerStars
- If 10% of importers play more ($10/month rake for site)
- 5,000 players × $10 × 12 months × 3% Pokza cut = $18K/year

**Not huge initially, but scales.**

### B. Hand History Database (B2B)

**Sell anonymized hand data to:**
- Research companies (universities, poker AI projects)
- Poker sites (for benchmarking)
- PokerTracker, Hold'em Manager (for their databases)

**Pricing:** $500-5,000/year for various licenses

**Year 3 deals:**
- 3 university research projects × $1,000 = $3K
- 2 poker sites × $5,000 = $10K
- 5 tool vendors × $1,000 = $5K
- Total: $18K/year

### C. Solver Integration Revenue

**GTO Wizard, PioSOLVER pay for integration placement.**
- Solver ad in app / solver hands featured
- Revenue share if users click

**Year 3:** $30-50K (estimates)

### D. Coaching Tools Integration

**Companies (HubSpot for coaches, Calendly, Stripe) pay for:**
- Integration plug (we support their API)
- Co-marketing (featured in our coach directory)

**Year 3:** $20-30K

### Total Partnerships (Year 3)

- Poker site referral: $18K
- Hand database licensing: $18K
- Solver integration: $40K
- Coaching tool integration: $25K
- **Total: $101K** (use $96K conservative)

## Risks to Business Model

### 1. **Coaching Saturation**
- What if too many coaches join → competition → prices fall → less revenue
- Mitigation: Verification, quality control, different tiers (beginner coaches earn less)

### 2. **Freemium Conversion Too Low**
- If free→premium conversion is 2% instead of 5%, revenue halves
- Mitigation: Improve product, email retention, better onboarding

### 3. **Sponsor Brands Can't ROI**
- Sponsors can't measure value, stop spending
- Mitigation: Detailed analytics, affiliate links, performance-based pricing

### 4. **Competition (Binked Copies Us)**
- Binked sees our success, copies premium + coaching
- Mitigation: Speed to market, network effects, coaching relationships

### 5. **Regulatory Issues**
- Poker becomes more regulated in some countries
- Mitigation: Operate in most liberal jurisdictions first, compliance team

### 6. **Churn Higher Than Expected**
- Users come back once per month (low engagement)
- Mitigation: Notifications, social features, leaderboards
